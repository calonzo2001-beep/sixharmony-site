Questions we get asked

What kinds of companies is this for?

Industrial Service Companies: inspection, repair, maintenance, and field service teams working on commercial sites. If your techs find problems on site that become quotes, and your office chases paperwork, it fits.

Can it handle emergency calls after hours?

Yes. It answers, asks the questions your dispatcher would ask, and pages your on-call tech for real emergencies with a written summary. Routine requests are booked or logged for the morning.

Does it work with our field service software?

It works alongside the tools you already use. Where your system allows connections, quotes and jobs flow in directly. Where it doesn't, the system sends clean summaries your office can enter in seconds. We check this on the strategy call.

Will it send quotes to customers on its own?

Only if you want it to. Most teams have it draft the quote from the tech's findings and a person approves it before it goes out. Follow-up on approved quotes then runs automatically until the customer answers.

What does it cost?

It depends on call volume and which workflows you want handled. After the strategy call you get a written scope with a setup fee and monthly price before anything is built.
