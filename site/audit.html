Questions about the audit

Is the audit really free?

Yes. No credit card, no obligation. You answer 12 quick questions and your Revenue Assessment shows on screen right away.

What do I get at the end?

Your Revenue Score out of 100 across five parts of your business, your #1 gap, an estimate of what that gap may be costing you each month, and a 30-day plan to close it. You can save or print it.

How accurate is the monthly estimate?

It's an estimate, not a promise. It uses your answers plus conservative industry averages, and the page shows exactly which averages. On the strategy call we replace the averages with your real numbers.

What happens with my answers?

They come to us so we can prepare for a strategy call if you book one. We don't sell or share them. Please don't include patient or customer information.

What is the strategy call?

A free 20-minute call where we walk through your assessment with you, check the numbers, and show you what fixing your #1 gap would look like. No slides, no pressure, and no long contract.
