Why we started

Most businesses don't lose customers because the work is bad. They lose them in the gaps: the call that rang out at lunch, the quote nobody followed up on, the patient who meant to book and forgot.

We started Six Harmony to close those gaps and help businesses maximize the revenue they're already earning. We work with two kinds of businesses, Eye Care Practices and Industrial Service Companies, because we understand how their calls and requests move and where the money slips through.

Our view is simple: one size fits none. Every practice and every shop runs differently, so every system starts with how your team already works. We take on a small number of installs at a time so each one gets built properly, tested with your staff, and reported on every month, with the calls answered, follow-ups sent, and the estimated revenue recovered laid out plainly. We do well when our clients do.
