Fair questions

Does it sound like a robot?

No. You can call the live demo and judge for yourself. The voice is natural, it handles interruptions, and callers can ask things the way they normally would.

What happens when it can't answer something?

It hands off. Clinical questions, pricing it isn't allowed to quote, or anything outside its lane goes to a person on your team with a written summary. It never guesses.

Yes. Your number stays yours. The system sits behind it, so patients and customers call the same number they always have.
