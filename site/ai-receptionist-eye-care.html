Questions we get asked

Will patients know they're talking to AI?

If they ask, it tells them. Most callers just want the appointment booked or the question answered, and the receptionist does that in a natural voice. You can call the live demo to hear it for yourself.

Can it book into our scheduling system?

It books into the calendar you already use where that system allows outside booking. Where it doesn't, it collects the full request and your front desk confirms the slot, so nothing is lost either way. We check this on the strategy call.

What about clinical questions or eye emergencies?

It never gives medical advice. Anything clinical goes to your team with a written summary. Callers describing sudden vision loss, flashes and floaters, eye injury, or chemical exposure are told to seek emergency care right away and your on-call contact is alerted.

Is it HIPAA compliant?

HIPAA-ready configurations are available, including a Business Associate Agreement, for practices that need them. We scope this before anything touches real patient information. The public demo uses fictional patients only.

What does it cost?

It depends on call volume and what you want handled. After the strategy call you get a written scope with a setup fee and a monthly price before anything is built. There's no long contract to start.
