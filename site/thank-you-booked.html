Why this matters now

Until recently, answering every call meant hiring more people or paying an answering service that takes a message and nothing else.

That changed. AI voice now holds a natural conversation, books into real calendars, and follows up by text and email for a small fraction of what another hire costs. The businesses that set it up first answer the calls their competitors miss.

Every week without it, the same calls ring out at lunch, after hours, and on weekends, and the same quotes and follow-ups sit unsent.

What we'll cover in 20 minutes

  1. Your Revenue Assessment, if you took the audit, and whether the numbers match what you see day to day.
  2. Where calls and requests slip through today: busy hours, after hours, follow-up, or quotes.
  3. What fixing your biggest gap would look like, step by step, and what it takes from your team.
  4. Whether it's a fit. If it isn't, we'll tell you.

  • A rough count of calls or requests in a normal week, and when they peak
  • Who answers the phone today, and what happens after hours
  • The software you use for scheduling, quoting, or invoicing. Names are enough.
  • Anyone else who would decide on this. They're welcome on the call.

Please don't bring patient or customer records to the call.

Common questions

Do we keep our phone number?

Yes. The system sits behind your current number, so patients and customers call the same number they always have.

What happens when it can't handle something?

It hands the call to your team with a written summary. It never guesses, and it never gives medical, legal, or technical advice.

Does it work with our software?

Where your scheduling or field service system allows outside booking, it books directly. Where it doesn't, it collects the full request and your team confirms it. We check this on the call.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope, so we don't quote before the call. The main factors are your call volume, which workflows you want handled, and which systems it connects to. You get a written scope with a setup fee and a monthly price before anything is built.

How long does it take?

Most installs are live about a week after the scope is agreed. We test it with your team before it handles a real caller.
